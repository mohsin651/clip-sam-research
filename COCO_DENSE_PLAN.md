# Frozen COCO dense-scene experiment

Written before COCO attribution inference. No SAM, training, attribution changes or ground-truth-dependent tuning.

- Official COCO 2017 validation images and train/val annotation archives from https://cocodataset.org/#download. Only validation instance masks are used; captions are unused.
- Verify all 5,000 images and decode every validation instance segmentation using pycocotools. Union all nonempty, successfully decoded masks per category, including valid crowd RLE masks. Malformed annotations are logged; an image with a malformed annotation is excluded from eligibility, not silently partially labeled.
- Use unchanged CLIP shortest-side 224 bicubic resize and center crop; masks use nearest-neighbor geometry. A category is meaningfully visible if its cropped union occupies at least 1% of the 224x224 image (502 pixels) and retains at least 25% of its area in the resized, uncropped mask. These are selection criteria, not attribution parameters.
- Require at least two distinct categories satisfying visibility. Sort eligible image IDs, permute with NumPy seed 42, take up to 500. Independently choose two distinct eligible category IDs without replacement using seed 42 in selected-image order. Save every eligible category and selection decision before inference. This tests multi-category scenes, not exclusively extremely crowded scenes.
- For each pair, prompt exactly `a photo of a {category}` with the actual COCO name, even when its grammar is imperfect. Reuse the same FP32 OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/16 checkpoint, full-channel attention, `explain` plus `spatial_maps` refined output. Check frozen ImageNet source/config/checkpoint hashes. No alternative attribution variants or tuning.
- Target GT is the category union. Other-category masks include all valid categories, even those too small to qualify as targets. Primary distractor mask = other-category union minus target mask, making target/distractor energy disjoint; also disclose inclusive other-category energy and overlap. All crop pixels are valid; unannotated/background pixels remain outside both masks.
- Reuse exact PG, raw-energy EPG, AP and image-macro foreground IoU implementations and the frozen mean-score threshold. Save raw target/distractor energy sums, total energy, normalized shares, and target/(target+distractor+1e-12). Also report target area and relative energy density to expose object-size effects. Zero/constant maps are retained and flagged.
- Prompt-switch diagnostics: Pearson correlation and cosine similarity of resized raw maps; L1 distance of unit-energy maps; each target's energy share under its own versus the other prompt; bidirectional switch success requires both own-prompt energy shares to exceed their cross-prompt values by >1e-8. Correlation alone does not establish semantic correctness. Undefined correlations for constant maps are recorded as missing with explicit counts.
- Objective diagnostic groups (not a verified semantic taxonomy): C good localization if PG=1, target_ratio>0.5 and IoU>=0.5; A correct target point/energy preference but coarse mask if PG=1, target_ratio>0.5 and IoU<0.5; B wrong-object/distractor OR unconfirmed target localization otherwise. Disclose B subtypes: peak in distractor, peak in background, target peak with distractor-dominated energy, degenerate map. A is only a potential boundary-refinement case: low IoU may also reflect incomplete object coverage.
- Main results average equally over image-target pairs. Bootstrap 2,000 resamples of whole images (seed 2026), keeping both prompts together; this respects within-image dependence. Also bootstrap switch outcomes and group proportions at image level. Freeze all rules before observing attribution scores.
- Save raw maps for all pairs. Generate 20 random pairs (seed 42), top 20 and bottom 20 sorted by PG then IoU, and 20 lowest target-ratio pairs. Tie-break by image ID/category ID. Groups may overlap; disclose selected IDs. Each example shows original, aligned crop, target GT, heatmap, overlay and exact binary mask. Save paired-prompt panels for every image, plus machine-readable switch scores.
- Report all required metrics, failure diagnostics, representative images, source/data hashes, selection counts, numerical failures and separate preparation/inference/reporting runtimes. Stop after this experiment.
